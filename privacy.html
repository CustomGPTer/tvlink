Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the TVLink app for Android (package uk.jj.tvlink) and this website. TVLink is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • There is no account, no adverts, no analytics and no crash reporting. The developer receives nothing from the app.
  • TVLink talks only to TVs on private addresses on your own home network. It makes no requests to the internet.
  • Saved TVs, pairings and settings are encrypted on your phone.
  • Check for updates and More apps open web pages in your browser, and only when you tap them.

Who the app talks to, and when

Only the TVs you choose, on your own network. To find them, TVLink sends a short local discovery message (SSDP and mDNS) that stays inside your home network. Commands go straight to the TV's private address. Some TV makers' local controls are not encrypted by design; TVLink uses encryption wherever the TV offers it, and remembers each TV's certificate so it notices if it ever changes. There is no TVLink server.

What stays on your phone

Your saved TVs, pairing approvals, layouts, favourites, activities and settings are kept in the app's private storage, encrypted with a key held in your phone's secure hardware. Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for TVLink. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

What the app can reach

Network access (Android calls it Internet, even for your home Wi-Fi), local network access on Android 17, Wi-Fi state (to find TVs), the infrared blaster if your phone has one, fingerprint or face unlock (only if you turn on the app lock) and notifications (only if you turn on the notification remote). No camera, microphone, location, contacts or media access.

Backups

Backups are saved only when you choose, to the place you pick, and are always protected with your password (AES-256). They leave out pairing keys, certificates, your PIN and private keys, so imported TVs pair again.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling TVLink removes everything it stored. To stop a TV accepting TVLink, remove it from the TV's own list of paired devices.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

TVLink collects nothing from anyone.
